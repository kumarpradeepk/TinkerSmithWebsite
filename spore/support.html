taking a photo spore can read

  • Use daylight and fill the frame with the mushroom, not the forest.
  • Shoot three angles: the top of the cap, the underside (gills, ridges or pores) and the stem down to its base.
  • Brush away leaves, but don't pick a mushroom you can't identify just to photograph it.
  • Use “Add angle” on a result to add another photo and run it again.

what the confidence means

The percentage is how strongly your photos match that species, weighed against close alternatives. A strong match still isn't proof — some species can only be separated by smell, spore print or a microscope. When Spore shows several possible matches or low confidence, treat the mushroom as unknown and potentially toxic.

look-alikes

Every result lists the species it could be confused with, especially toxic ones, and how to tell them apart. If a dangerous look-alike is possible, Spore says so — even when it's unlikely.

“no mushroom found” or a failed identification

This usually means the photo is too dark, blurry or distant, or the connection dropped. Your photos stay on screen; try again on a better connection or retake the shot closer up.

field-guide photos

Spore shows community photographs only when their licence allows reuse. When none is available you'll see an illustration, clearly labelled as artwork rather than a photo of the species.

free use & spore premium

Free use includes a few identifications and expert questions each day; the counter resets daily. Spore Premium (monthly or yearly) removes those limits. Subscriptions are billed and managed by Apple:

  • Restore: open the paywall in Spore and tap “Restore”, signed in with the Apple Account you used to subscribe.
  • Cancel: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Spore, or apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions.
  • Refunds: request them from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

your data

Your field book lives on your device — there's no account and no cloud copy, so we can't restore it if your phone is lost or erased. Your iCloud device backup may include it. To erase everything, use Settings → Erase all data in the app. Full details are in the Privacy Policy.

accessibility

Spore supports VoiceOver, larger text and Reduce Motion; the scan animation becomes a calm cross-fade when Reduce Motion is on. Tell us if anything is hard to use.
