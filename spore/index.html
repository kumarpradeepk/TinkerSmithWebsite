01 · in the field

a photo in. a careful answer out.

spore is built for the moment you crouch beside something strange on the forest floor — and for every quiet minute of reading afterwards.

a photo, then a name

shoot the cap, the underside and the stem. spore reads the features and gives you its best match with an honest confidence — or tells you plainly when it isn't sure.

look-alikes first

every result is checked against the species it could be confused with. the dangerous twins are named, compared side by side, and never hidden.

a living field guide

morphology, habitat, season and taxonomy, with community photographs from GBIF and Mushroom Observer — shown only when their licence allows.

your own field book

save finds, add notes, keep your scans. it all lives on your phone — no account, no cloud copy of your collection.

02 · how spore reads a mushroom

01 · the cap

shape before colour

dome, funnel, bell or parasol — the silhouette narrows thousands of species to a family long before colour does.

02 · the details

warts, rings, a cup

veil remnants, a skirt on the stem, a sac at the base. small things, and often the ones that matter most.

03 · the texture

pits, ridges, pores

a honeycomb cap or a sponge of pores tells a different story from blade-like gills.

04 · the danger

the deadly twins

some of the most toxic mushrooms on earth look ordinary. spore always checks them — and says so.

05 · the answer

a name, with care

the likely species, the confidence behind it, and a reminder you'll see on every result: a guide, not a guarantee.

03 · the honest part

a guide, not a guarantee.

no app can tell you a wild mushroom is safe to eat — and spore never will. it is here to help you look closer, learn faster and ask better questions of a real expert.

  • never eat a mushroom on an app's word. confirm every identification in person with a qualified local expert.
  • doubt is shown, not hidden. low confidence and several close matches are said out loud.
  • if you think someone has eaten something toxic, contact your local emergency number or poison centre immediately.
