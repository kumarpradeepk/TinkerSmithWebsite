pinch — sodium tracker & salt coach

privacy policy

effective august 11, 2026 · tinkersmithstudio

The short version: your food log never leaves your phone. Pinch has no accounts, no analytics, no ads, no trackers and no cloud database. We couldn't read what you ate for lunch if we wanted to — and we don't want to.

1. what we collect

Nothing, by default. Everything you enter in Pinch — foods, sodium amounts, budgets, streaks and settings — is stored only on your device, as local app data. We operate no server that receives this data, and the app works fully offline.

2. what stays on your device

  • Foods and "usual suspects" you save, with their sodium values
  • Your daily sodium budget and everything you log against it
  • Streaks, weekly history and trends
  • Reminder times, appearance and theme settings

3. no third-party SDKs

Pinch ships with zero third-party SDKs — no analytics, no advertising, no crash-tracking services that phone home. If that ever changes, this policy will be updated first and the change called out in the release notes.

4. notifications

Meal check-ins are scheduled locally on your device. Notification content is composed on-device and shown only on your screen; enable "hide previews" in your device settings if you don't want it visible on the lock screen.

5. your controls

  • Edit or delete any logged entry, any time
  • Erase all data from within the app with one confirmed tap
  • Deleting the app deletes all local data

6. children

Pinch is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect any data from them (or anyone).

7. changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date. Material changes will be called out in the app's release notes.
