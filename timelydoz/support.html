Notifications and reminders

TimelyDoz needs notification permission to alert you about scheduled doses. Open iPhone Settings → Notifications → TimelyDoz and allow notifications. If a reminder is missing, also check that the medication is active and has at least one saved dose time.

TimelyDoz Pro and purchases

TimelyDoz is free to use with one active medication and full reminders. TimelyDoz Pro unlocks unlimited active medications, follow-up nudges, editable meal times, and all-time history. Subscription prices, billing period, and any introductory offer are shown before you confirm a purchase in the App Store.

To restore a previous purchase, open the TimelyDoz Pro screen and choose Restore Purchases. Apple manages payments, cancellations, and refunds through your Apple ID subscription settings.

Backup and privacy

Your medication records and dose history are stored on your device. You can export a backup when you choose and import it later. For more details, read the TimelyDoz Privacy Policy.

Medical information

TimelyDoz is a reminder and organization tool. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, dosage recommendations, or medication-interaction checking. Always follow the directions from your doctor, pharmacist, and medication label.
