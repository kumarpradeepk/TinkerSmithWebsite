diary
one tap, five to undo

your usuals live on the home screen. tap → logged, haptic, done — with a five-second undo. no spinner, no confirm dialog, ever.

the working, shown

every pour prints its maths — ml × abv × 0.789 ÷ your country's gram. a "unit" is 8 g in the uk, 14 in the us, 20 in japan. you get the number you already know.

dry days are banked

an empty day proves nothing — you bank a dry day on purpose, and it lands in the calendar in moss. a slip pauses a streak; it never resets one.

quiet by design

on-device, no account, face-id lock — and lock-screen nudges that never say the word "alcohol". speaks 20 languages with one honest voice.

weeks that improve

the statistics screen hands down a verdict, not a scold — trend against last week, your steadiest day, and the money the dry ones gave back.

cost and calories

every pour is priced and measured as you log it. the round you did not buy turns into a number you can spend somewhere better.

themes that stay subtle

warm, dark and monochrome. nothing on screen announces what the app is for — including the icon sitting on your home screen.

wrist and home screen

log the usual from your watch without reaching for the phone, and keep today and the week on a widget — a glance, not an app launch.

the party trick

every number shows its maths.

no black boxes. the add sheet prints the derivation under the result while you type, so the accuracy nerds — our people — can check us on a beer mat.

and the divisor is yours: the same pour is 1.3 units at who's 10 g, 1.6 units in the uk, 0.9 standard drinks in the us.
